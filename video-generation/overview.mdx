Vivix Blazing Fast Video Generation lets you produce polished AI avatar videos on demand without a live session. Instead of streaming in real time, you submit a generation task, wait for it to complete, and receive a playback URL for the finished video. Choose this product line when you need a distributable video file — for pre-recorded content, marketing assets, educational modules, or any use case where your viewers don't need to influence what the avatar does while they watch.

## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit a Generation Task">
    Send a `POST` request to `/v1/video-generation/tasks` with your chosen model and inputs — text for the avatar to speak, audio to drive the lip sync, or images to incorporate into the video. The API responds immediately with a `task_id` and a `pending` status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Poll for Completion">
    Send periodic `GET` requests to `/v1/video-generation/tasks/{task_id}` to check the task status. The status moves from `pending` through `processing` and finally resolves to either `completed` or `failed`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fetch the Playback URL">
    When the status is `completed`, the response includes a `video_url` field containing the URL of the finished video. Use this URL to play or distribute the generated video in your application.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Models

Vivix Video Generation supports two model lines, giving you a choice between speed, quality, and visual style.

| Model | Line | Characteristics |
|---|---|---|
| `vivix-a1-lite` | A1 | Fastest generation times; ideal for prototyping and high-volume pipelines where turnaround speed matters most |
| `vivix-a1` | A1 | High-quality output; best for production assets where visual fidelity is the priority |
| `vivix-w1` | W1 | World-style video generation; produces richly contextualized scenes with expressive avatar performance |

<Note>
  All three models share the same task submission and polling endpoints. You switch between them by changing the `model` field in your request body.
</Note>

## Input Types

Every generation task accepts one or more of the following input types, which you include in the request body when submitting a task:

- **Text** — Provide a script and the avatar will speak it, with lip sync generated automatically.
- **Audio** — Supply an audio file to drive the avatar's lip sync directly, using your own recorded voice or a text-to-speech output.
- **Images** — Include images to incorporate visual content into the generated video.

You can combine inputs in a single task — for example, pairing a text script with a custom avatar image — depending on the model and your use case.
