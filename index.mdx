## Product Lines

Vivix ships two production-ready product lines. Both share the same base URL, the same API key authentication, and the same `{code, message, data}` response envelope — only the integration pattern differs.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Streaming Avatar" icon="broadcast-tower" href="streaming-avatar/overview">
    Real-time avatar sessions driven over a WebSocket control channel. The avatar talks, listens, performs actions, sings, and dances — all live. Use this when your users need to influence what happens on screen while they watch it.

    **Models:** `vivix-a1-stream`, `vivix-a1-stream-lite`
  </Card>
  <Card title="Video Generation" icon="film" href="video-generation/overview">
    Asynchronous, task-based video production. Submit your content over REST, poll for completion, and serve the playback URL. No real-time session required.

    **Models:** `vivix-a1-lite`, `vivix-a1`, `vivix-w1`
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Get Started in Three Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your Vivix account">
    Register at [platform.vivix.ai](https://platform.vivix.ai) and sign in to your developer account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your API key">
    Open **API Keys** in the developer platform, create a new key, and store it securely on your server — never expose it to the browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build">
    Follow the [Quickstart](quickstart) to make your first API call, then choose the product line that fits your use case.
  </Step>
</Steps>
